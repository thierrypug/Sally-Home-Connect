As simple as an app, as private as your home

Most smart homes go through a brand's servers. Sally does the opposite: everything happens between your phone and the computer in your home.

Your data stays at home

Sally creates no account and sends nothing over the Internet. What happens in your home is nobody's business but yours.

No technical setup

Sally guides you step by step to add a bulb, a plug, a shutter or a radiator. You answer by voice or with a tap, and it does the rest.

No subscription

No monthly fees, no service that shuts down overnight. If the Internet goes down, your home keeps working.

A home that acts on its own

You tell Sally what to do, and when. Then it takes care of it without you having to think about it.

  • At sunset, the shutters close.
  • When the door opens at night, the hall light comes on.
  • If there is a water leak, Sally warns you and switches off the plug.
  • In the morning, the heating is already in comfort mode.

Your energy use, made clear

Sally adds up what your radiators and plugs use, and gives you the cost in euros at your electricity price, off-peak hours included.

  • The power being used right now.
  • The total for the day, the week and the month.
  • The breakdown device by device.

How it works

Sally is software. You install it on a computer that stays on in your home, and you control it from any screen in the house.

  1. Install Sally

    Try it for free on a Windows computer. To live with it, the final version will run on a Raspberry Pi, a small box that stays on day and night.

  2. Plug in a USB stick

    A Zigbee stick, an EnOcean stick, or both. It talks to your devices by radio.

  3. Add your devices

    Sally recognises them and guides you, by voice if you like. Then you open the app on your phone, at the address sally.local.

Features

Everything Sally does

One app for the whole house, on phone, tablet and computer. Every feature works without the Internet.

The features, one by one

Lights and plugs

Switch on, switch off and dim with a single gesture. One button turns the whole house on or off at once.

Roller shutters

Raise, lower or stop each shutter, or all of them together. Sally can close them by itself at sunset.

Heating

Electric radiators with pilot wire: comfort, eco, frost protection or off, room by room, with the power used.

Scheduled heating

Choose the comfort hours for each day of the week. The rest of the time, Sally turns the heating down by itself.

Routines

“When… if… then…”: at a set time, at sunrise or sunset, or when a sensor reacts.

Scenes

“I'm leaving”, “Movie night”, “Good night”, “Wake up”: several devices set with a single button. You can create your own.

Energy and cost

Consumption right now, for the day, the week and the month, device by device, with the cost in euros.

Home mode

Home, away, night or holiday. On holiday, Sally can switch on a few lamps in the evening so the house looks lived in.

Sensors

Temperature, motion, door opening, water leak, smoke: Sally shows their status and uses them in routines.

Alerts and log

Leak, smoke, low battery, a device that stops responding: Sally reports it and keeps a record of what happened.

Backup

Your whole home fits in a single file. It lets you reinstall everything without redoing the settings.

Your way

Five colour themes, four display sizes and five languages: French, English, Spanish, Italian and German.

Voice control

“Turn on the living room light”, “close the shutters”, “movie night”: Sally understands your voice without the Internet. Nothing you say leaves the house. When you add a device, it even asks you out loud which room to put it in. In French only for now.

Linked switches

A battery-free switch can control a shutter, a lamp or a plug. Sally asks you which one, you press its buttons, and it's linked.

A built-in guide

The app's Help tab explains everything, topic by topic: adding a device, talking to Sally, creating a routine, backing up your home.

Room by room

Each room has its own picture and devices. The picture lights up when a lamp is on there, and darkens when everything is off: you see the state of the house at a glance.

  • One tap on a tile switches on or off.
  • Whatever is on is always shown in amber.
  • If a device doesn't respond, Sally tells you within seconds.

What about the Internet?

Sally doesn't need it. A single setting, off by default, allows it to look up one piece of public information.

Off by default

As long as you don't turn it on, Sally never accesses the Internet. Everything else works exactly the same.

The weather, if you want it

Once allowed, Sally shows the weather for your town. Only the location of the chosen town is sent, nothing about your home.

Try Sally for free

The Windows beta is available: a full 30-day trial, no account and no credit card. A demo is included so you can explore everything without any hardware.

Compatibility

What you need

Sally is sold on its own, with no hardware. You choose your devices from two major wireless families: Zigbee and EnOcean.

Required hardware

A computer at home

A Windows 10 or 11 PC that stays on. A Raspberry Pi version is on its way.

A Zigbee USB stick

It talks to Zigbee bulbs, plugs, modules and sensors. Sally is developed with the Sonoff ZBDongle-P stick.

An EnOcean USB stick

It talks to EnOcean modules and switches, often battery-free. Sally is developed with the EnOcean USB 300 stick.

Just one of the two sticks is enough. Sally recognises the stick by itself when you plug it in.

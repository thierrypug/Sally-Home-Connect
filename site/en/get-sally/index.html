Get Sally

Try Sally for free

The Sally beta for Windows is available. You get a full 30-day trial, no account and no credit card. A demo is included so you can explore everything, even without hardware.

Download the Windows beta Become a tester

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 80 MB. The file is published on GitHub, where you'll also find the installation guide and what's new in each version.

Become a tester

Choose your email: a ready-made message opens, with a few questions about your home and your hardware. Fill it in and send it, that's all. Nothing is stored on this site.

Or simply write to sallyhomeconnect@gmail.com

Install Sally in three steps

Nothing else to install: everything Sally needs is in the file.

  1. Run the installer

    Double-click the downloaded file. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose “More info” then “Run anyway”: the beta is not signed yet.

  2. Open Sally

    The desktop icon opens Sally in your browser. The first time, it shows a warning: that's normal, Sally has its own certificate. Choose “Continue”.

  3. Add your devices

    Plug in your Zigbee or EnOcean stick: Sally guides you, by voice if you like. Without hardware, open “Sally Home Connect - Démonstration” (the demo) from the Start menu.

Windows to try it, Raspberry Pi to live with it

The Windows beta is for discovering Sally. To adopt it, the final version will run on a Raspberry Pi: a small, silent box that stays on day and night, with no screen or keyboard.

Then a licence, not a subscription

After the trial, Sally is activated with a licence made for your installation: you pay once, with no subscription. The price will be announced at launch.
